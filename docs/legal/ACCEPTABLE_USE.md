# Acceptable Use Guidelines for TeleCaller AI

**Last Updated:** September 25, 2026  

---

## 1. Transparent & Consensual Recording

- **Inform Callers:** Always inform all parties at the beginning of the call that the conversation is being recorded and may be transcribed.
- **Respect Objections:** If a participant does not consent to being recorded, immediately discontinue recording or exclude that conversation from automated pipelines.

---

## 2. Sensitive Information Safeguards

- **Protect Credentials & Financials:** Avoid transcribing calls containing sensitive payment credentials (credit card numbers, CVVs), passwords, PINs, or confidential healthcare records unless your business implements strict compliance controls.
- **Secure Exports:** Ensure any Google Sheets or Drive folders receiving transcripts have strict access controls and are not shared publicly.

---

## 3. Verification of AI Output

- Do not make binding legal, employment, or disciplinary commitments based solely on automated sentiment scores or AI call summaries without human review.
- Always cross-check audio playback when accuracy is essential.

---

## 4. Responsible Device Storage

- Periodically archive or delete old recordings from your device storage to free up space.
- Enable device lock screens (PIN, biometric) to prevent unauthorized local access to your recorded audio files.
