## 4. Information We Access, Collect, and Process

### 4.1 Google Account Information
- **What is collected:** When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your full name, email address, profile picture URL, Google account ID, and OAuth tokens.
- **Why it is needed:** To authenticate your identity and authorize requests to Google Drive and Google Sheets on your behalf.
- **How it is processed & stored:** Stored locally on your device using Android Encrypted Storage. Your credentials are never uploaded to our servers.
- **Sharing:** OAuth tokens are transmitted only to Google's servers to execute authenticated API calls.
- **Control:** You may revoke permissions at any time via [Google Account Permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

### 4.2 Call Recording Audio Files
- **What is accessed:** Audio files of telephone calls stored in your device storage that you grant access to via Android's Storage Access Framework or MediaStore.
- **Why it is needed:** To play back, organize, transcribe, and back up your recordings.
- **Server Storage:** Audio files are **never** hosted or permanently stored on our servers. Recordings remain on your device unless you explicitly trigger:
  1. **Google Drive Backup:** Uploaded directly to your personal Google Drive account.
  2. **AI Transcription:** Sent directly to the configured AI Transcription Provider API for speech-to-text processing.
- **Background Processing:** If you enable the automatic background pipeline, queued recordings are processed according to your explicit configuration settings.
- **Control:** You have complete control over folder selection, file deletion, and upload toggles.

### 4.3 Call Metadata
- **What is collected:** File name, file size, duration, timestamps, caller/callee name, phone number, and call direction (incoming/outgoing) matched from your device call logs.
- **Why it is needed:** To correlate audio recordings with specific callers, organize conversation logs, and populate exported spreadsheets.
- **Storage:** Metadata is cached locally on your device in SQLite/AsyncStorage and written to your personal Google Sheet if Sheet Sync is enabled.

### 4.4 Generated Transcripts and AI Insights
- **What is generated:** Text transcripts, call summaries, action items, customer sentiment, and deal stages.
- **Processing:** Transcripts are generated by sending audio snippets to the configured AI API (e.g., Google Cloud Speech-to-Text / configured LLM).
- **Storage:** Transcripts and summaries are stored locally in your device database and synced to your personal Google Sheet or Google Drive folder upon your request.

## 5. Android Device Permissions

The Application requests specific Android runtime permissions:
- **Audio / Storage Access (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` / Storage Access Framework):** To discover, read, and play back call recordings from your designated folder.
- **Call Log (`READ_CALL_LOG`):** To match call recordings with contact names, phone numbers, timestamps, and call directions.
- **Contacts (`READ_CONTACTS`):** Optional, to resolve caller names for an organized address book view.
- **Post Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** To notify you of pipeline completion, background processing status, and transcription results.
- **Foreground Service (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`):** To perform file scanning, transcription, and Drive uploads reliably without interruption when the app is in the background.

## 6. Third-Party Services and Data Disclosures

The Application integrates directly with external APIs:
- **Google Identity & OAuth Services:** Authenticates user identity without exposing passwords.
- **Google Drive API (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`):** Grants access **only** to files and folders created by the Application (restricted scope). The app cannot view or modify other personal files on your Drive.
- **Google Sheets API (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets`):** Appends call metadata, transcripts, and analytics into a dedicated spreadsheet created in your account.
- **AI Transcription & Language Model APIs:** Processes audio into text. No audio or transcript data sent to your configured API is sold or rented to third-party data brokers.

---

## 7. Data Security and Retention

- **Local Security:** Tokens and sensitive configuration keys are protected using Android's Keystore-backed Encrypted Storage.
- **Data Retention:**
  - Device audio files remain on your device until you delete them.
  - Cached transcripts and metadata remain in local storage until you clear app cache, sign out, or uninstall the app.
  - Cloud backups are retained in your personal Google Drive and Sheets under your sole ownership and control.
- **Account Disconnection:** Signing out immediately clears all local authentication tokens and active session caches.
