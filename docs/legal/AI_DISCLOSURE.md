# Privacy & AI Processing Disclosure for TeleCaller AI

**Last Updated:** September 25, 2026  

---

## 1. What Data Does TeleCaller AI Access?

1. **Call Audio Files:** The app accesses audio files stored in your selected folder to allow playback, transcription, and backup. Your audio is **never stored on our servers**.
2. **Call Information:** Timestamps, phone numbers, contact names, and call duration are read from your device call log to correlate recordings with contacts.
3. **Google Account Access:** If you sign in, we request access to your basic profile, Google Drive (to back up your audio into an app-specific folder), and Google Sheets (to sync call records).

---

## 2. How Does AI Transcription Work?

- When you initiate transcription or enable the background processing queue, audio snippets are submitted to the designated Speech-to-Text API.
- Transcripts and summaries are generated automatically and saved back to your device and your linked Google Sheet/Drive.
- **AI Limitations:** Automated transcripts are drafts and may contain inaccuracies. Please review critical details manually.

---

## 3. How Is Your Data Kept Private?

- Your audio files remain on your device and inside your personal Google Drive.
- We do not sell your personal data, audio recordings, or transcripts to third parties or data brokers.
- You can revoke access at any time through your device settings and Google Account security dashboard.
